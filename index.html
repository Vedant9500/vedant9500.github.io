About

I got into programming through machine learning, then got pulled into the systems underneath it: the solvers, the benchmarks, and the tools that tell you how things actually work (or why they’re breaking).

Most of my projects are just me learning by building from scratch. Sometimes that’s math-heavy stuff like Glassbox (recovering equations from raw data) or FMMS (simulating gravity with the Fast Multipole Method). Other times it’s tools I wanted for my own workflow, like Hark (a native Linux command palette), WTF (natural language search for shell commands), or OpenLMlib (shared memory for coding agents).

I like taking black-box ideas and building them out until I can actually inspect them.
