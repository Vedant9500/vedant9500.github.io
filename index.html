About

I got into programming through machine learning, then got annoyed at how much of it was a black box and went looking at the systems underneath. Now I mostly write Rust, C++, and Python, building solvers, benchmarks, and tools from scratch.

I care about results I can reproduce. If a number moves, I want to know why before I move on. Most days I’m on Arch, usually one config change away from a broken desktop.

Outside the terminal I’m into F1 and motorsport sims, fighter jets, rocket engines, and CFD. Mostly I just like simulating things that are fast, hot, or explosive.
